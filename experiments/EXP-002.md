# 🔬 Experiment EXP-002: Optimized Transfer Learning with BN Freeze & Swish Bottleneck

- **Experiment ID:** EXP-002
- **Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Model Family:** MobileNetV2 / EfficientNetV2-B0 (ImageNet Pretrained)
- **Target Task:** 3-Class Categorical Fish Eye Freshness Classification (`fresh`, `highly_fresh`, `not_fresh`)
- **Baseline Accuracy (EXP-001):** 69.0%
- **Target Accuracy:** **88.0% – 94.0%**
- **Status:** Designed & Implemented in `train_freshness_classifier.py` v2.5

---

## 1. Experiment Question
Can addressing the five core failure modes identified in EXP-001 (BN parameter corruption, head variance shift, biological augmentation distortion, aspect squashing, and hard target cross-entropy) elevate validation accuracy from 69% to above 88%?

---

## 2. Hypotheses
- **H1 (BN Freezing):** Explicitly locking all `BatchNormalization` layers in inference mode (`layer.trainable = False`) during Phase 2 fine-tuning prevents ImageNet feature degradation caused by small-batch noise.
- **H2 (Head Simplification):** Replacing the 820k parameter 3-layer head with a single 128-unit Swish bottleneck (`GAP -> Dropout(0.35) -> Dense(128, swish) -> Dropout(0.2) -> Dense(3, softmax)`) eliminates train/test variance shift and drastically reduces overfitting on small datasets.
- **H3 (Biology-Safe Augmentations):** Switching to `fill_mode="reflect"`, limiting rotation to $15^\circ$, bounding brightness to $[0.90, 1.10]$, and removing vertical flips will eliminate synthetic cataract streaks and preserve true pupil opacity signals.
- **H4 (Aspect-Ratio Preservation):** Preprocessing with square center-cropping before $224 \times 224$ resizing will preserve the spherical roundness of the pupil and cornea convexity.
- **H5 (Label Smoothing):** Adding `label_smoothing=0.10` will prevent overconfident gradients on continuous, transitional samples between `Highly Fresh` and `Fresh`.

---

## 3. Dataset & Preprocessing Configuration
- **Input Dimensions:** $224 \times 224 \times 3$
- **Cropping:** Square center crop (`smart_center_crop`) to prevent anisotropic warping.
- **Normalization:** $[-1, 1]$ native scaling.
- **Augmentation Pipeline:**
  - `rotation_range`: $15^\circ$ (was $40^\circ$)
  - `width_shift_range`: $0.10$ (was $0.25$)
  - `height_shift_range`: $0.10$ (was $0.25$)
  - `zoom_range`: $[0.92, 1.08]$ (was $0.3$)
  - `horizontal_flip`: True
  - `vertical_flip`: False (was True)
  - `brightness_range`: $[0.90, 1.10]$ (was $[0.7, 1.3]$)
  - `fill_mode`: `"reflect"` (was `"nearest"`)

## 4. Model Architecture & Training Strategy

### 4.1 Architecture
```
Input: (224, 224, 3)
  ↓
MobileNetV2 / EfficientNetV2-B0 (ImageNet Pretrained)
  ↓
GlobalAveragePooling2D(name="avg_pool")
  ↓
Dropout(0.35, name="top_dropout")
  ↓
Dense(128, activation="swish", name="dense_features")
  ↓
Dropout(0.20, name="feature_dropout")
  ↓
Dense(3, activation="softmax", name="predictions")
```
- **Trainable Head Parameters:** 164,355 (down from ~820,000).

### 4.2 Two-Phase Training Strategy
- **Phase 1 (20 Epochs):**
  - Base model fully frozen.
  - Head trained with Adam ($\text{LR} = 5\times 10^{-4}$).
  - Loss: `CategoricalCrossentropy(label_smoothing=0.10)`.
  - Class weights applied.
- **Phase 2 (40 Epochs):**
  - Top 50 convolutional layers unfreezed.
  - **All BatchNormalization layers explicitly frozen** (`layer.trainable = False`).
  - Adam ($\text{LR} = 1\times 10^{-5}$).
  - Callbacks: `EarlyStopping(patience=8)`, `ReduceLROnPlateau(factor=0.5, patience=3)`.
