## 9. Interpretation & Limitations
- **Fact:** On this new test split and baseline preprocess, the shipped weights score **38.07% accuracy / 0.29 macro F1**, with almost no recall on `not_fresh`.
- **Fact:** This is **not** the historical ~69% EXP-001 number. That claim remains historical and is still not independently reproduced (original split missing).
- **Do not** treat 38.07% as proof that the historical 69% was wrong, or as a v2.5 result. Different split, no original val set, possible original class-index or crop mismatch remain untested here.
- Production inference still center-crops; this evaluation did not measure that serving path.
- Class-index alignment with the original training run is **assumed** from the manifest mapping (`fresh=0`, …); it is not embedded in the `.keras` archive.
