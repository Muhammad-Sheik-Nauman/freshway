# Experiment EXP-007: species-balanced sample weighting

- **Experiment ID:** EXP-007
- **Status:** `MEASURED; NOT PROMOTED`
- **Parent:** EXP-006 production configuration
- **Objective:** Address species-specific errors without changing the validated EXP-006 model or preprocessing.

## Change

Training uses one sample weight per image:

`normalized(species inverse-frequency weight * EXP-006 freshness class weight)`

Species weights are calculated from the training split only:

`total_train_images / (number_of_species * species_train_images)`

The existing freshness class weights are preserved and multiplied into the species weights. Validation and test records receive no training weights and are not used for fitting. The fixed `baseline_split_v1` manifest remains unchanged.

## Preserved EXP-006 Configuration

- MobileNetV2 ImageNet backbone and v2.5 classification head
- RGB direct nearest-neighbor resize to 224x224, no crop, MobileNetV2 `preprocess_input`
- Existing reflect/bounded-brightness augmentation
- Batch size 32
- Adam learning rates `5e-4` and `1e-5`
- Label smoothing `0.10`
- Existing early stopping, ReduceLROnPlateau, and best-checkpoint callbacks
- BatchNormalization freezing during fine-tuning

## Isolation

- Trainer: `server/training/train_exp007_species_balanced.py`
- Output directory: `server/models/experiments/exp007_species_balanced_mobilenetv2/`
- Best checkpoint: `freshness_exp007_best.keras`
- EXP-006 and the original baseline model are not output targets.

## Results

- **Test set:** 654 images from the fixed `baseline_split_v1` manifest.
- **Overall accuracy:** **66.67%** (436/654), versus EXP-006 **69.42%** (454/654).
- **Macro F1:** **0.655661**, versus EXP-006 **0.684028**.
- **Per-class F1:** Fresh **0.548813**, Highly Fresh **0.761165**, Not Fresh **0.657005**.
- **60% threshold:** 326/654 accepted (**49.85% coverage**) with **80.37% accuracy** among accepted predictions, versus EXP-006 403/654 (**61.62%**) and **81.39%**.
