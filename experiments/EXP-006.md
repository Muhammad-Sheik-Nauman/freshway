## 1. Experiment Question
Does the committed v2.5 trainer (BN freeze, Swish-128 head, biology-safe aug, label smoothing 0.10) beat the existing `freshness_model_best.keras` on the **same** `baseline_split_v1` test set?

---

## 2. Hypothesis
v2.5 + ImageNet init + `[-1, 1]` nearest-resize matching EXP-005 will exceed 38.07% test accuracy. **Not assumed true until test eval exists.**

---

## 3. Dataset & Split
- Unchanged source tree + existing `experiments/manifests/baseline_split_v1.json`

## 4. Method
- Script: `server/training/train_from_manifest.py`
- Backbone: MobileNetV2 ImageNet; v2.5 Swish head from `train_freshness_classifier.build_model`
- Preprocess: RGB, nearest 224×224, no crop, `preprocess_input` `[-1, 1]`
- Train aug: v2.5 reflect / bounded brightness (on `[0,255]` then preprocess)
- Saves **only** under `server/models/experiments/exp006_v25_mobilenetv2/`
- Does **not** write `freshness_model_best.keras`

## 5. Results

- **Test accuracy:** **69.42%**
